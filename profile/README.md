## Current Projects

### [the-great-home-firewall](https://github.com/ninecent-oss/the-great-home-firewall)
The ultimate Deep Packet Inspection (DPI) and traffic shaping system designed to prioritize what matters.

- **Status:** ⚙️ Queued for Development
- **License:** GNU GPL v3.0
- **Loyalty-Based QoS:** Automatically boosts internet speeds for **Weixin (WeChat)** and **QQ** traffic to 110% of your bandwidth.
- **Harmony Enforcement:** Intelligent packet dropping for unauthorized protocols such as **Telegram** and **WhatsApp**. 
- **Focus:** If it's not green (微信) or a penguin (QQ), does it even need to be fast?
